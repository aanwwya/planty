# v1 has no automatic photo identification

For v1, species are chosen manually from a list. Uploading a photo does not trigger automatic identification. Photos remain part of a Plant's record and growth history.

This avoids depending on an identification provider, its accuracy, its cost, and its failure modes before the care loop is proven, and it keeps the door open to any provider later. The cost is a harder onboarding step for users who do not know their species, and the quality of the species list and its care guidance becomes a first-class product concern.
