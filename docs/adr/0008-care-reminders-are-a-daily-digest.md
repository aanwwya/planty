# Care reminders arrive as one daily digest

Rootly sends one daily digest at a local time chosen by the Owner, naming the Plants that are due and grouping them by care action. The in-app due list is always available, including when notification permission is denied.

Per-action notifications were rejected because an Owner with many Plants would receive a stream of interruptions for a low-urgency task. The cost is that an urgent-looking reminder cannot be sent immediately; the digest is the only push in v1.
