# Plants are private by default; public Profiles are minimal

Every Plant is private until its Owner explicitly makes it public. A Profile becomes discoverable only when it has at least one Public Plant. A public Profile exposes the Owner's display name, avatar, coarse area, and Public Plants (personal name, photo, and Species). Care logs, schedules, Measurements, notes, and Growth Entries are never public in v1.

This is a privacy-by-default decision: care activity is a proxy for when someone is home or away, and once location-linked content is exposed it cannot be reliably un-exposed. The cost is that the discovery experience is thinner and must earn attention through the quality of public plant cards rather than activity feeds.
