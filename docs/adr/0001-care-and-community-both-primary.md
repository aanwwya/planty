# Care and community are both primary in v1

Rootly could launch as a care-first utility with social as a later layer, as a social-first discovery app, or as a dual-product. We chose to treat plant care and community discovery as equally primary from day one, because the product's premise is that caring for your own plants and seeing other people's plants are two halves of the same experience, not a feature and its add-on.

Consequences: v1 must define and ship a minimum viable social loop (map, profiles, Public Plants, and a global recent feed) alongside the care loop, which raises scope, moderation, and location-privacy requirements. Neither loop can be deferred to make the first release smaller. The launch plan must create local density for the social side while the care side remains useful with zero network effects. Both loops remain the product's positioning, but v1 validates care retention first; discovery is read-only and may be sparse.
