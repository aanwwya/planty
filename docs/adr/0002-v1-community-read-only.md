# v1 community is read-only discovery

The community side of v1 is limited to exploring a map, viewing public profiles, and viewing public plant collections. There are no follows, reactions, comments, or direct messages.

This keeps v1 free of message moderation, harassment handling, and real-world coordination risks, and tests whether discovery alone is valuable before building interaction features. The cost is that "connecting with other plant lovers" is, in v1, only viewing: retention on the community side must come from the quality and freshness of public collections, not from social feedback loops. Adding interactions later will require new domain concepts and a moderation model, but does not invalidate the discovery model.
